About Us

Sankalp school situated at Manneguda Sagar highway in Hyderabad, the pride of Telangana state welcoming the students from playgroup to X, CBSE Day- Residential.

The school established in 2017 and being constructed on more than four acres landscape with student friendly environment. It lays full emphasis on full development of the children which enables them to achieve great heights. Transport facility provided for the day scholars and semi residential students.
